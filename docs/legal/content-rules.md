# Rules for public reader wikis (draft)

> Draft, not in force. See [README.md](README.md). Replace every `[BRACKETED]` part.

Private worlds are your own business. These rules apply to what you publish in a public reader
wiki, where anyone can read it.

## Do not publish

- Other people's writing, art or maps without the right to use them. That includes text from
  rulebooks that is not in an open licence: for Dungeons & Dragons, only the SRD (with its required
  attribution) may be reproduced.
- Sexual content involving anyone under 18, real or fictional.
- Sexually explicit or graphically violent material without [AN ADULT-CONTENT MARKING; decide
  whether to allow it at all].
- Hatred or harassment aimed at real people or groups.
- Real people's private information.
- Anything illegal in [JURISDICTIONS].

## Reporting a page

Every public wiki has a "Report this wiki" link. Reports go to [ABUSE EMAIL]. Tell us the address
and what is wrong.

## What we do with a report

1. We acknowledge it within [72 hours].
2. If the page plainly breaks these rules or the law, we close the wiki (the author's private
   world is untouched) and tell the author why.
3. For a copyright complaint we need: who you are, what work is yours, where it appears, and a
   statement that you have not allowed that use. The author can reply; we restore the wiki if the
   complaint is withdrawn or not pursued within [14 days].
4. Repeated or serious breaches end the account.
