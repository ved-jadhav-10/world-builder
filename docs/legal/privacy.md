## What we do not do

- We do not sell your data or show advertising.
- We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. The one cookie we set keeps you signed in. A
  public reader wiki sets a second cookie only if it is password-protected, to remember that you
  gave the password. Your theme, music settings and reading progress are kept in your own browser
  and never sent to us.
- We do not use your work to train AI models, and we ask AI crawlers to stay out of public wikis.
